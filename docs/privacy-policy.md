# Magnets Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-03-23_

Magnets is a private shared-memory app for creating small invite-based spaces (“Magnets”), posting moments, and viewing those moments in widgets.

## Summary
- We collect only the information needed to run the app’s core features.
- We do **not** sell personal data.
- Private content is used to provide the product experience you request.
- Some features rely on Apple services (such as widgets, notifications, and potentially CloudKit) and backend infrastructure (currently Supabase) to function.

## Information We Collect
Depending on how you use Magnets, we may collect:

### 1. Account / identity data
- App-specific identifiers
- Device identifiers required for app functionality
- Invite codes or membership information for shared spaces

### 2. Content you create
- Magnet names
- Posts, captions, reactions, and other in-app content
- Photos or media you choose to upload

### 3. Device / app operation data
- Push notification tokens
- Diagnostic or error information needed to keep the app working
- Widget refresh and sync metadata

### 4. AI / agent-related content
If you use AI-assisted or agent-assisted features, the text or content you submit for those features may be processed to generate a result inside the app.

## How We Use Information
We use collected information to:
- create and maintain shared spaces
- sync content across the app and widgets
- deliver notifications and widget updates
- support invite / join flows
- improve reliability, performance, and safety
- provide AI-assisted or agent-assisted product features when requested

## Sharing of Information
We may share limited data with service providers that help us run the app, such as:
- **Apple** for platform services such as notifications, widgets, and device-level app functionality
- **Supabase** for backend database / function infrastructure
- other infrastructure providers strictly as needed to operate the service

We do not sell your personal data.

## Data Retention
We keep data for as long as needed to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms.

## Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No internet service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

## Children’s Privacy
Magnets is not intended for children under 13.

## Your Choices
You can choose whether to:
- upload photos or other media
- enable notifications
- use AI-assisted features

You may also request deletion of your data by contacting us.

## International Use
If you use Magnets outside the country where it is operated, your information may be processed in other jurisdictions.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after updates means the updated policy applies.
