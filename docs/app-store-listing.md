# Magnets — App Store Listing Draft

## App Name

## Subtitle

## Promotional Text
Create tiny private spaces for the people who matter, post moments instantly, and keep them alive on your Home Screen with interactive widgets.

## Description
Magnets is a private shared-memory app for keeping people close.

Create invite-based spaces, post photos and moments, and pin them to beautiful widgets so the people and memories you care about stay visible all day.

### What you can do
- Create private shared spaces (“Magnets”)
- Invite people with simple codes and deep links
- Post moments, reactions, and quick updates
- See activity directly in your widgets
- Use quick widget actions like Wave and Send Love
- Keep shared memories visible without opening a social feed

### Why Magnets is different
Most social apps are built for timelines, audiences, and noise.
Magnets is built for small, private, meaningful connection.

It’s closer to a shared emotional dashboard than a public feed.

### Built for fast, lightweight connection
- Widget-first experience
- Private by design
- Small-group sharing instead of public broadcasting
- Designed for everyday closeness, not content performance
